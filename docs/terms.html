Terms of use

Last updated September 16, 2026

Job Poller Alerts is a personal project operated by Akshaj Bharadwaj. It checks public career pages and emails matching new postings to a configured recipient. There is no public registration or paid subscription.

Informational alerts

Listings may change, close, or be missed. Delivery and availability are not guaranteed. Verify each role and apply through the employer’s official application page. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the employers whose public listings it checks.

Authorized use

Only connect an email account you own or are authorized to use, and only send alerts to recipients who have agreed to receive them. Google access can be revoked at any time through your Google Account settings.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for data-handling details. For questions, contact akshaj.bharadwaj@gmail.com.
